# Neural Training Mode - Gameplay Concept

## Core Loop
1. Player makes political decisions
2. Neural Core simulates outcomes and generates procedural stories
3. AI learns from choices → adapts future events and empire behavior
4. Offline evolution via simple neuro-simulation

## Integration with Procedural Story Generator
Import generateProceduralStory() and trigger on major decisions.
